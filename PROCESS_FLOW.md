# Fabrication Process Flow: Silicon Penetrating Neural Probe

Design-stage process flow for a single-shank silicon probe with platinum recording sites, matching the layout generated by `neural_probe_layout.ipynb`.

> **Status: design document, not a record of fabricated devices.** No step below has been fabricated by the author unless stated in the "Author's RIE experience" section. Every value marked **[VERIFY]** is a placeholder that must be replaced by a cited literature value or by the recipe of the target facility before it appears in a paper.

## 1. Overview

| Item | Choice |
|---|---|
| Substrate | SOI wafer, device layer thickness = shank thickness (default 15 um, see `neural_probe_buckling.ipynb`) |
| Shank | 60 um wide, 5 mm long, tapered tip (see layout notebook) |
| Insulation | PECVD dielectric below and above the metal |
| Metal | Adhesion layer + platinum, patterned by lift-off |
| Sites | 8 recording sites, 15 um diameter, optional coating (TiN, IrOx, PEDOT) |
| Release | Deep reactive ion etching (DRIE) of the outline, then release from the handle wafer |

## 2. Mapping from layout layers to process steps

| GDS layer | Layout content | Process step |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Sites, traces, pads (metal) | Photolithography + metal deposition + lift-off |
| 3 | Passivation openings (sites, pads) | Photolithography + dielectric etch (RIE) |
| 1 | Probe outline | Photolithography + DRIE of the device layer, then release |

| # | Step | Purpose | Key parameters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wafer clean | Remove organics and particles | Solvent clean, O2 plasma or piranha **[VERIFY facility rules]** |

## 4. Critical points to discuss in the paper

- **Tip geometry:** sidewall angle and tip sharpness from DRIE affect insertion force (see buckling notebook).
- **Etch selectivity:** the dielectric opening etch (step 9) must stop on platinum; the outline DRIE (step 13) must stop on the buried oxide.
- **Mask erosion and scalloping:** both limit the minimum shank thickness and sidewall quality.
- **Residual stress:** PECVD film stress can curl a thin released shank.
- **Notching at the buried oxide:** a known DRIE effect on SOI; mention mitigation **[VERIFY]**.
